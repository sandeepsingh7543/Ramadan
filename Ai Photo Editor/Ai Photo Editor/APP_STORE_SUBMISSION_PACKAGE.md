AI Photo Editor – Smart Edit is a powerful, privacy-first photo editing app that works entirely on your device. Edit photos with AI-powered tools, professional filters, and precise adjustments. All processing happens locally – your photos never leave your device.

AI-Powered Tools

• Enhance Photo - Auto brightness, contrast, saturation boost
• Blur Background - Professional blur effects
• Smart Fix - One-tap photo enhancement
• Brighten/Darken - Quick brightness adjustments

Professional Filters
• Original, Black & White, Vintage, Cinematic
• Warm, Cool, Sepia, Vivid
• Real-time preview for all filters

Precise Adjustments
• Brightness, Contrast, Saturation
• Exposure, Sharpness, Highlights, Shadows
• Real-time slider controls

Creative Effects
• Blur Background - Gaussian blur
• Vignette - Professional vignetting
• Noise Reduction - Clean up grainy photos

Transform Tools
• Crop - Multiple aspect ratios (1:1, 4:3, 16:9, Free)
• Rotate - 90° rotation
• Flip - Horizontal and vertical flip

Essential Tools
• Undo/Redo - Full edit history
• Reset - Restore original photo
• Save - Export to photo library
